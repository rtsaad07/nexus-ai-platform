How Modern Large AI Models Work

From raw Transformer attention matrices to Mixture-of-Experts and Chain-of-Thought Reinforcement Learning.

1

Transformers & Attention

LLMs process text by converting tokens into high-dimensional vector embeddings. Multi-Head Attention mechanisms allow models to dynamically weigh relationships between words regardless of distance.

2

Mixture of Experts (MoE)

Instead of activating all parameters for every token, MoE routes tokens to specialized sub-networks ("experts"). This achieves trillion-parameter performance at a fraction of active compute cost.

3

Reinforcement Learning (RLHF/RLAIF)

Base models are aligned using Reinforcement Learning with Human Feedback (RLHF) or AI Feedback (RLAIF) to ensure safety, helpfulness, and conversational alignment.

4

CoT Reasoning Engine

Reasoning models (like DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI o3) utilize test-time compute—generating explicit step-by-step thinking tokens before finalizing output to solve complex math, code, and logic.

Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)

AGI represents autonomous systems that demonstrate cognitive abilities across all human economically valuable tasks—learning, reasoning, adapting, and innovating without domain-specific re-training.

Level 1: Emerging
Equal to unskilled human

Level 2: Competent

Reasoning models capable of multi-step code and mathematical discovery.

Level 3: Expert
